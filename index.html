About the data

2014

This is a simple, undirected graph of U.S. domestic flights from OpenFlights. Airports whose country is “United States” and that have a valid IATA code are nodes; a route in either direction becomes one edge. Repeated airline routes and self-loops are removed. The explorer includes airports with at least one domestic route. The route snapshot dates to 2014, so this is a study of network structure, not a current flight schedule.
